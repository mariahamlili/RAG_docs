Support for wine industry impacted by red grape oversupply

Certain Australian wine regions are experiencing an oversupply of red wine grapes.

If you are an affected grape grower, there is a range of support you may be eligible to access.

Australian Government support programs

Farm Household Allowance program

Help with income support, a health care card and other allowances.

Rural Financial Counsellors

Free and confidential financial counselling to improve your financial situation.

RIC (Regional Investment Corporation)

Improve the long-term resilience and profitability of your eligible business with concessional loans.

Farm Management Deposits Scheme

Help to deal more effectively with fluctuations in cash flows.

Age Pension Extended Land Use Test

Exempt all the land on a single title, based on your eligibility for the Age Pension.
