Join us in 2027

ABARES Outlook conferences have been informing and shaping Australian agriculture’s policy agenda for more than 50 years, bringing together government figures, industry leaders, policymakers and expert researchers.

Planning is in full swing for next year’s ABARES Conference, taking place 2-3 March 2027 at Hotel Realm Canberra and online.

The event will offer dynamic session formats, hybrid participation options, and features a new networking dinner for relaxed networking, conversation, and connection.

Registrations are expected to open late October.
