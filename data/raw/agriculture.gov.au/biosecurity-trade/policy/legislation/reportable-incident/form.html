Reportable biosecurity incidents form

Use this form if you are responsible for goods or a vehicle (aircraft or vessel) carrying goods that are under biosecurity control or an exposed goods order.

You must submit this form if you become aware of a reportable biosecurity incident involving the goods. This report is required under the Biosecurity Act 2015.
