Biosecurity Matters newsletter

Don't miss the next edition of Biosecurity Matters.

Subscribe to receive the next edition direct to your mailbox.

Biosecurity Matters features news about biosecurity including pest and disease discoveries, changes to biosecurity policies and processes, events and interesting finds from our airports, seaports and mail centres. The newsletter also spotlights the important work that our biosecurity staff are doing.

The quarterly e-newsletter is an important source of news for importers and exporters as well as those who work in biosecurity.

This newsletter provides a greater understanding of our department's work in managing biosecurity risks overseas, at the border and within Australia.
