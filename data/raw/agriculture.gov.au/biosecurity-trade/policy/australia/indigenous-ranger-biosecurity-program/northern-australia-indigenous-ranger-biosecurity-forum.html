Northern Australia Indigenous Ranger Biosecurity Forum

The Indigenous Ranger Biosecurity Program (IRBP) helps protect Australia’s more than $81 billion agricultural industries by partnering with Indigenous rangers in northern Australia to undertake biosecurity surveillance for exotic pests, diseases and weeds. A key element of the IRBP is capability building initiatives like the Northern Australia Indigenous Ranger Biosecurity Forum.

The forum brings up to 200 Indigenous rangers from remote communities across northern Australia together with trainers and support staff from agencies that share an interest in biosecurity.  It is the only community forum of its type in northern Australia and provides valuable face-to-face delivery of targeted biosecurity training, awareness and educational activities, knowledge-sharing and networking opportunities for Indigenous rangers.

Each forum is delivered in partnership with an Indigenous stakeholder via a grant opportunity.

In addition to biosecurity, the agenda covers topics such as conservation and land management, remote field skills, traditional knowledge and leadership.

The Ranger Forum:

  • provides a key opportunity to increase biosecurity skills and knowledge in the Indigenous ranger network
  • facilitates Indigenous-led workshops, presentations and sessions, helping rangers learn from each other for stronger learning outcomes
  • engages Indigenous rangers in unique practical activities
  • fosters two-way learning between Indigenous rangers, DAFF scientists and other stakeholders, blending traditional ecological knowledge with western science
  • connects a diverse network of Indigenous rangers who are passionate about working on country in northern Australia
  • establishes lasting partnerships and friendships and fosters pride and collegiality amongst rangers
  • supports aspirations of Indigenous ranger groups and motivates and empowers rangers
  • provides an opportunity to be on-Country in northern Australia, welcomed by traditional owners and following cultural protocols

2025 Ranger Forum

The 2025 Northern Australia Indigenous Ranger Biosecurity Forum was hosted by the Carpentaria Land Council Aboriginal Corporation (CLCAC). Attendees were welcomed to Moungibi Country in Burketown, Queensland, by Traditional Owners, the Gangalidda Garawa People.

The forum featured tailor-made biosecurity workshops, hands-on activities, and yarning circles.

One of the highlights of the forum were the insights shared by BirdLife Australia on the ancient flyways of migratory shorebirds, gathered from more than 20 years of studies.

2023 Ranger Forum

The 2023 Northern Australia Indigenous Biosecurity Ranger Forum was organised and hosted by the Mandubarra Aboriginal Land and Sea Incorporated at Kurrimine Beach, Queensland, from 17 to 19 October. The forum brought together 268 Indigenous Rangers, coordinators and biosecurity staff for 3 days of hands-on and immersive workshops, yarning circles and cultural experiences.
