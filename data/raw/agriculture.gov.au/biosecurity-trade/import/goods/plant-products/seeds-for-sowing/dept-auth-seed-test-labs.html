Department authorised seed pathogen testing laboratories

The Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (the department) has reviewed Australia’s policies for testing tomato and capsicum seed. As a result of this review, Australia’s seed testing policy has been updated by establishing a list of authorised laboratories registered to undertake testing of tomato and capsicum seed for Tomato brown rugose fruit virus (ToBRFV) and Tomato mottle mosaic virus (ToMMV).

From 12 November 2025, laboratories that intend to test tomato and capsicum seed for export to Australia must register to be a department authorised testing laboratory. Laboratories can register by completing a registration form confirming the revised testing requirements can be implemented.
