Registered inorganic fertiliser supply chains

Reduced risk classification

Offshore manufacturers of inorganic fertiliser which meet the department’s eligibility and application requirements may be approved to hold a reduced risk classification with the department. The requirements to apply for a reduced risk classification are outlined in the inorganic fertiliser policy documents.

Consignments of inorganic fertiliser originating from a manufacturer that holds a reduced risk classification that also meet the relevant importing conditions set by the department will be subject to reduced biosecurity intervention on arrival.

The department maintains a list of holders of reduced risk classifications that have consented to their details being published on the department’s website. These manufacturers are outlined in the tables below:
