The agriculture, fisheries and forestry sectors face long-standing challenges in attracting, retaining and 
developing a sufficient workforce, worsened by tight labour market conditions across the economy. These 
sectors are vital for local economies and provide thousands of jobs in rural and regional Australia.
To ensure the continued success and growth of the agriculture industry, the Australian Government is 
committed to supporting the agricultural workforce by investing in the skills of Australians, creating more job 
opportunities in regional areas and supporting secure pathways for overseas workers.
Work we do
We work collaboratively with industry and unions to ensure the short, medium and long-term workforce needs 
of agriculture are understood and considered across government. 
Agricultural Workforce Forum
The Agricultural Workforce Forum (the Forum) brings together government, industry and unions to discuss 
agricultural workforce-related issues. The Forum allows direct collaboration with employer groups and unions 
on matters that impact agricultural workers.
The Forum has provided government with input on many issues, including the Regional Migration Review, Food 
Supply Chain Capacity Study, regional housing and the Agricultural Trade Apprenticeship scoping study. For 
more information, visit Agricultural Workforce Forum.
Agricultural workforce projects
National Farm Safety Week
National Farm Safety Week is an annual campaign delivering critical farm safety information and awareness 
aimed at reducing injuries and fatalities on farms. Read more at Farmsafe.
AgConnections – Skilled Agricultural Work Liaison pilot program
The AgConnections – Skilled Agricultural Work Liaison pilot program aims to encourage university students to 
consider a career path in agriculture. The pilot program will provide funding for universities to develop and run 
an agricultural work liaison pilot program over the 2025-2026 calendar years.
AgCAREERSTART
The AgCAREERSTART pilot program is a structured employment program to help young Australians start a 
career in agriculture in a gap year. Read more about AgCAREERSTART at agcareerstart.com.au.
Community Perceptions and Worker Experiences Program
The Community Perceptions and Worker Experiences research program aims to better understand and respond 
to community perceptions and worker experiences within the agriculture, fisheries and forestry sectors. For 
more information, visit Community Perceptions and Worker Experiences Program.

Government engagement 
We engage closely with other Commonwealth agencies to understand and represent agricultural workforce 
issues and ensure they are considered in relevant policies. Below are some of the key policies we engage on. 
Overseas workforce
The Pacific Australia Labour Mobility (PALM) scheme is a key program for meeting workforce shortages 
in unskilled, low-skilled and semi-skilled positions in the agriculture sector, by enabling eligible Australian 
businesses to hire workers from 9 Pacific islands. For more information, visit PALM scheme.
The Migration Strategy outlines a new vision for Australia’s migration system. For more information, visit 
Migration Strategy.
Food Supply Chain Capacity Study
The Jobs and Skills Australia Food Supply Chain Capacity Study is due for release in early 2025 and will provide 
advice to government on how training, education and migration systems can work together to meet agricultural 
workforce needs. For more information, visit Food Supply Chain Capacity Study.
Fee-Free TAFE in Agriculture
Fee-Free TAFE provides opportunities for Australians to access fee-free training in areas of skill needs, including 
in agriculture. Over 20,300 students have enrolled in an agriculture-related Fee-Free TAFE course as at 30 June 
2024. Find out more about these opportunities at YourCareer.
