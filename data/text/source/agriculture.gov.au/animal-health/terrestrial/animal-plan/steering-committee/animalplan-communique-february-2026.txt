Project #72: CalfWays, Sustainable Dairy Calf Management 
Roadmap
This initiative aims to transform the management of dairy 
calves in Australia by 2035. It serves as an overarching 
framework to unify and strengthen existing initiatives by 
the dairy and beef industries and drive further innovation 
across both industries in sustainable calf management.

Project News – November 2025 to January 2026
Updated projects
• #27: Emergency animal disease crisis management planning 
o The crisis management planning guidance materials are in the final stages of review. The project committee has agreed to hold an 
additional workshop in the first half of 2026 to support industry testing and upskilling.
• #44: Australia’s Animal Sector Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) Action Plan 2023 to 2028
o The AMR Task Group will continue for another 12 months. 
o A business case for sustainable funding to undertake national AMR priority actions and initiatives in the animal health sector is being 
developed for June 2026.
o A monitoring and evaluation framework for the AMR Action Plan was completed and will be used to monitor progress towards the plan’s 
objectives.
• #34: Agricultural Traceability Enhancement: Australian Government contribution – The Traceability Grants Program – Round 4 was open from 12 
January 2026 to 18 February 2026. The grant round will fund projects that meet the program objectives and focus on the strategy’s priority areas 
for action that require further investment, being:
o Align processes and technologies to capture and use data for multiple purposes, such as regulators and feedback to farmers 
o Support industry to implement interoperable systems 
o Implement a communication and education campaign to strengthen awareness of agricultural traceability. 
• #64: Assessing the feasibility of testing trapped pooled insects for lumpy skin disease (LSD) virus
o Work has commenced at the Australian Centre for Disease Preparedness (ACDP) to explore the feasibility of testing pooled mosquito 
samples as a potential diagnostic tool to support response activities in the event of an LSD outbreak in Australia.
o Initial laboratory work has focused on optimising sample preparation and testing methods.
o Preparatory work is also underway for the next phase, which will involve determining the lowest amount of virus that can be reliably 
detected in pooled mosquito samples.
