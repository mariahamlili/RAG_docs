Project News – February to April 2026
Updated projects
• #27: Emergency animal disease crisis management planning
o The project will end on 30 June 2026.
o All crisis management plan templates and basic guidance videos are complete, and a final workshop will be held in May 2026.
• #44: Australia’s Animal Sector Antimicrobial Resistance Action Plan 2023 to 2028
o Australia’s Animal Sector AMR Action Plan 2023-2028 is undergoing a mid-term review.
o Risk based identification of 10 priority drug-bug-host combinations for surveillance is nearly complete.
o Dairy Australia will soon finalise antimicrobial resistance testing to estimate susceptibility profiles for E. coli (Escherichia) and Enterococcus 
species in dairy cattle.
• #64: Assessing the feasibility of testing trapped pooled insects for lumpy skin disease virus – Mosquitoes have been bred, raised and prepared for 
testing.
o Pools of mosquitoes will be tested to understand the lowest level of contamination with lumpy skin disease virus that can be detected.
• #66: Australian Animal Disease Genomics Initiative: building the molecular blueprints for animal health – The Australian Animal Disease Genomics 
Initiative ran a second request for project partnership proposals to generate foundational genomic data for priority animal pathogens, parasites 
and viromes.

Project News – February to April 2026
Updated projects
• #67: Enhancing rapid decision-support for emergency animal disease outbreaks - HASTE – Phase one of the project has progressed to the final 
delivery and consolidation stage.
o Core deliverables have been completed, including modelling workflows, advancements in TAPPAS-2 capability and delivery of two 
simulation workshops.
o Engagement and collaboration with government and industry are ongoing to increase awareness and use of HASTE tools.
o Final reporting is underway, with potential next-phase activities under consideration.
• #72: CalfWays, Sustainable Dairy Calf Management Roadmap to 2035 – The CalfWays website is now live.
o Organisations are encouraged to subscribe to the quarterly newsletter and explore opportunities to support or collaborate 
on CalfWays initiatives.
