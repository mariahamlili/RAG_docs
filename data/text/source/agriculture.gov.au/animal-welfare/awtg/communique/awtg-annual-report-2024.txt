Animal Welfare Task Group (AWTG) - Annual Report 2024
Renewal of the Australian Animal Welfare Strategy
Highlights:
●
A subgroup of the AWTG was formalised in August 2024 to discuss issues 
around implementation of the poultry standards into state and territory 
legislation. 
●
The subgroup is chaired by Vic. and met 5 times in 2024 to discuss 
implementation of the standards, with the aim of national harmonisation.
●
NSW and WA have undertaken public consultation on proposed regulations 
to implement some aspects of the poultry standards including standards 
relating to requirements for cage housing for laying hens.
●
WA completed the first of a two-stage process implementing the poultry 
standards by way of regulation. The WA Animal Welfare (Poultry) Regulations 
2024 implement the standards relating to the phase out of conventional layer 
hen cages, the standards providing breeding ducks access to water and a 
range of standards with commencement date of 1 July 2025 in the poultry 
standards.

Highlights:
●
Commonwealth, state and territory agriculture ministers confirmed their 
commitment to jointly lead the renewal of the Australian Animal Welfare 
Strategy (AAWS), and to develop a National Statement on Animal Welfare 
during 2024.
●
A first tranche of public consultation was completed in mid-2024 and is 
available here: Get involved - DAFF. This engagement identified a range of 
challenges in Australia’s system, but also opportunities, and feedback 
informed next steps in the strategy’s renewal.
●
A second tranche of stakeholder consultation focused on the development of 
a National Statement on Animal Welfare. The National Statement on Animal 
Welfare was developed in 2024 and endorsed by all Agriculture Ministers in 
December.
●
Governance was established, including a Strategy Advisory Group and cross-
portfolio Sponsor Group with the Department of Health and Aged Care and 
the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water, in 
recognition that the responsibility for animal welfare lies across various 
agencies.
Project Information:
●
The Australian Government has allocated $5 million over four years to renew 
the AAWS, aiming to establish a comprehensive national framework for 
animal welfare in Australia.

Agriculture ministers are working towards national endorsement of

Animal Welfare Task Group (AWTG) - Annual Report 2024
Highlights:
●
Members of AWTG and the Stakeholder Advisory Group (SAG), as well as 
targeted stakeholders provided substantial feedback on drafts of the 
standards throughout 2024.
●
A revised draft is being developed for wider public consultation, which is 
planned for 2025.
Project Information:
●
At the February 2020 Agricultural Ministers’ Meeting, ministers agreed that 
Queensland, on behalf of ATWG, would lead the development of new 
Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at 
Processing Facilities and Establishments.
●
The standards and guidelines will replace the previous Model Code of 
Practice for Livestock at Slaughtering Establishments which was finalised in 
2001.
●
This work is being informed by an independent scientific literature review 
published in 2022, feedback from meetings of the SAG held throughout 2022 
and visits to livestock processors in 2023. The SAG includes industry bodies, 
animal welfare groups, animal welfare scientists and consumer groups.

Animal Welfare Task Group (AWTG) - Annual Report 2024
Examination of animal welfare issues related to virtual 
fencing technology to support a harmonised approach for 
its use
Lead jurisdiction: Commonwealth
Timeframe: planned for finalisation in 2025
Highlights:
●
In 2024, AWTG agreed to develop an Australian Animal Welfare Guide for 
Virtual Fencing as the preferred approach for harmonisation.
●
The subgroup working on this project established a Stakeholder Reference 
Group (SRG), which consisted of agricultural and animal welfare scientists 
and representatives from agricultural industry groups, animal welfare 
organisations and the veterinary industry.
●
The SRG met 6 times in 2024 and meetings were chaired by an independent 
facilitator. 
●
Drafting of the guide is underway.
Project Information:
●
In August 2021, the Agriculture Senior Officials’ Committee (AGSOC) referred 
a national examination of issues associated with virtual fencing technology, 
with a view to harmonising regulation across jurisdictions, to the AWTG for 
progression.
●
An AWTG subgroup, chaired by the Commonwealth, comprising 
representatives from NSW, WA, Qld, SA and Vic, conducted this work.
●
An independent scientific literature review on animal welfare considerations 
for virtual fencing and an addendum were published in 2023 and underpin 
the development of the guide. The addendum captures new, and more 
detailed information on virtual fencing technology since the finalisation of 
the literature review. 
Highlights:
●
Targeted stakeholder consultation occurred in early 2024 to seek feedback 
on the staged approach to improvements to the standards and guidelines 
development process.
●
The AWTG is working towards finalisation of the standards development 
framework and jurisdictional lead process map. 
●
Substantial updates are planned for the AWTG webpage in 2025 to provide 
greater transparency to stakeholders regarding the standards development 
process. 
●
Further work to improve the process for developing national animal welfare 
standards and guidelines will continue in 2025.
Project Information:
●
At the December 2022 Agriculture Ministers’ Meeting, ministers requested 
that AGSOC consider options to improve the process for developing 
nationally consistent animal welfare arrangements, to ensure clarity, 
harmonisation and timeliness.
●
A subgroup of the AWTG was convened, consisting of representatives from 
NSW, WA, Qld, Vic. and the Commonwealth as secretariat, to explore options 
to be provided to agriculture senior officials.
●
Ministers discussed options progressed by agriculture senior officials and 
noted a staged approach for improvement. They have tasked agriculture 
senior officials with continuing work, focusing on practical elements. This 
work is being progressed by the AWTG.
Lead jurisdiction: Commonwealth
Timeframe: currently in progress, finalisation timeline to be determined
