Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock – horses

AWTG 26-03 discussed updates regarding the Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock.

Delivery of the AWTG workplan

AWTG discussed developments regarding the delivery of the AWTG workplan, and its approach to

inform state and territory senior officials.

Pig Welfare

AWTG discussed jurisdictional engagement with Australian Pork Limited on their industry project to

improve the welfare of pigs.

Virtual Fencing Technology

AWTG discussed the finalisation of the virtual fencing project, including the approach to informing

state and territory senior officials of the project’s completion.

Implementation of the Australian Animal Welfare

Standards and Guidelines for Poultry into state and

territory legislation

AWTG 26-03 noted an update from jurisdictions on their progress towards implementation of the

poultry standards.

Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)

AWTG 26-03 noted an update from the Commonwealth on progress to develop the Australian Animal

Welfare Strategy, including progress on the development of the six animal group chapters, with a focus

on targeted stakeholder engagement on the chapters on animals in the wild, companion animals and

animals used for work, sport and recreation.
