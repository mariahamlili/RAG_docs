Suitability of horses under existing Australian Animal

Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Land Transport of

Livestock

A total of 63 submissions were received during the consultation period for the consultation regulatory

impact statement (CRIS) on improving horse welfare during land transport. Collation of feedback is

underway.

Virtual Fencing Technology

The final report for the literature review on the animal welfare implications of virtual fencing

technology has been completed and is under consideration by the subgroup, who will now convene a

meeting to discuss next steps.
