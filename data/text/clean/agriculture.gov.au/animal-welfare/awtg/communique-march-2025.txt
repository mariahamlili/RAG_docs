Options for the Development of National Standards and

Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at

Processing Facilities and Establishments

AWTG 25-02 noted an update from Queensland that jurisdictions continue to work collaboratively on

the third draft of the Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at Processing

Facilities and Establishments.

Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock - horses

AWTG 25-02 noted an update from Queensland that ministers continue to work towards national

endorsement of amendments to the Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines – Land

Transport of Livestock to improve the welfare of horses.

Virtual Fencing Technology

AWTG 25-02 noted an update from the Commonwealth that members of the stakeholder reference

group (SRG) are in the process of providing feedback on the draft Australian Animal Welfare Guide for

Virtual Fencing.

Implementation of the Australian Animal Welfare

Standards and Guidelines for Poultry into state and

territory legislation

AWTG 25-02 noted an update from Victoria on next steps for resolving cross-jurisdictional

implementation issues.

Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)

AWTG 25-02 noted an update from the Commonwealth that a second round of consultation for the

AAWS is underway. This has commenced with a ‘Have Your Say’ survey to test whether the priority

areas for action identified under the previous AAWS remain important for a renewed, modern, and

future-focused AAWS. Consultation with key stakeholders for the Livestock and Production and Aquatic

Animals AAWS chapters is commencing in April, with AWTG continuing its governance role.
