Options for the Development of National Standards and

Guidelines for Animal Welfare

AWTG 24-02 noted an update from the secretariat that targeted stakeholder engagement to discuss the

draft standards development framework will now commence.

Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at

Processing Facilities and Establishments

AWTG 24-02 noted an update from Queensland that the review of stakeholder feedback of the draft

standards is almost complete. Preparation for public consultation is underway. Work has commenced

on procuring a consultant for regulation impact analysis.

Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock - horses

AWTG 24-02 noted that the proposed amendments to the Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock to improve the welfare of horses will be presented for endorsement to

agricultural senior officials at the upcoming meeting in June. The amendments will then be brought to

agriculture ministers for final endorsement.

Virtual Fencing Technology

AWTG 24-02 noted an update from the Commonwealth that an independent facilitator for the virtual

fencing stakeholder reference group has been engaged. Meetings will commence shortly.

Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)

AWTG 24-02 noted an update from the Commonwealth that all ministers have agreed to a joint

leadership approach to renew AAWS and to develop a national statement on animal welfare. Broad

stakeholder consultation on the AAWS has commenced, with a Have Your Say and submissions process

open, and further avenues for engagement planned.
