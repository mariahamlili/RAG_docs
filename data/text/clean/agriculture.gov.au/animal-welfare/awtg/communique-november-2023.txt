Options for the Development of National Standards and

Guidelines for Animal Welfare

AWTG 06-23 noted an update that a subgroup of the AWTG have developed a stakeholder engagement

approach and a draft standards development framework as part of the staged approach to

improvement. Initial engagement with key stakeholders is planned for early 2024.

Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at

Processing Facilities and Establishments

AWTG 06-23 noted an update from Queensland that the substantial volume of feedback on the draft

standards and guidelines provided by members of the Stakeholder Advisory Group and AWTG

members continues to be assessed. AWTG discussed the approach to national regulation impact

analysis.

Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for

Land Transport of Livestock - horses

AWTG 06-23 noted an update from Queensland that the Decision Regulatory Impact Statement has

progression of the amended standards through AGSOC and AMM for national endorsement is

underway.

Virtual Fencing Technology

AWTG 06-23 noted an update from the Commonwealth that the literature review has been published

on the AWTG webpage. The virtual fencing subgroup is being reconvened to progress next steps.

Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)

AWTG 06-23 noted an update from the Commonwealth on progress in renewing the AAWS, with work

continuing to focus on developing a stakeholder engagement strategy. Stakeholder consultation will

commence soon and the Commonwealth acknowledged the importance of working closely with states

and territories on the renewal of the AAWS.
