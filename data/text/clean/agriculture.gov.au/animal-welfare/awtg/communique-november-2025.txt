Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at

Processing Establishments

AWTG 25-06 noted an update from a cross-jurisdictional working group which is continuing to

consider options to progress development of the processing standards, which is being led by

Queensland.

Pig Welfare

AWTG discussed further details provided by Australian Pork Limited about its project to improve the

welfare of pigs.

Virtual Fencing Technology

A revised version of the Australian Animal Welfare Guide for Virtual Fencing will soon be circulated to

AWTG members for endorsement. The revisions incorporate additional feedback provided by

jurisdictions.

Implementation of the Australian Animal Welfare

Standards and Guidelines for Poultry into state and

territory legislation

AWTG noted updates from jurisdictions on current progress to implement the poultry standards into

state and territory legislation.

Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)

AWTG noted an update from the Commonwealth on stakeholder engagement activities undertaken in

2025 for the development of the Livestock and Production and Aquatic Animals chapters of the

Australian Animal Welfare Strategy.
