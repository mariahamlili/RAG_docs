Agricultural emissions inhibitors (AEIs) including methane and nitrification inhibitors, can help reduce

agricultural emissions. Interest in these products is growing in Australia and internationally.

International regulators are beginning to include some of these products in agricultural, veterinary and

feed additive regulations.

In Australia, we are considering how AEIs could support broader environmental and sustainability

goals. To do this, we need to understand how current regulatory frameworks apply, and if they remain

fit-for-purpose.

We have prepared a discussion paper which outlines potential approaches for regulating methane- and

nitrification-inhibiting products.

The discussion paper is open for consultation until 5 pm (AEST) on Wednesday 1 July 2026.
