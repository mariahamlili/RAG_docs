Industry Engagement Team

Scope of work

A central point of contact for agriculture, fisheries, forestry food and fibre stakeholders. The team works with other Commonwealth agencies and state governments to ensure industry perspectives inform decisions. For all enquiries on specific industry issues relating to biosecurity, imports, exports and trade, please use the relevant contact details at our contact us page.
