Applications closed

The program adopts a challenge-based approach that supports innovative solutions to address complex and multi-dimensional challenges imposed by drought and climate impacts. It will fund projects that address one of the following challenges:

- Advancing Natural Capital and Biodiversity for Drought Preparedness and Resilience

- Innovating Water Management for Enhanced Drought Resilience in Australian Agriculture

- National Innovation for Enhancing Community Resilience to Drought in Australian Agricultural Regions

Grant recipients will investigate the effectiveness of high-impact and evidence-driven innovations, that have achieved proof of concept, over two years to increase drought resilience.

Projects will use a consortium of project partners that include farmers or farmer-led organisations.

Drought Resilience Innovation Challenges Pilot Program recipients have been announced.
