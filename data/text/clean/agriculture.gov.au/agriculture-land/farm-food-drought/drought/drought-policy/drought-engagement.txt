We share information and seek feedback on drought-related policy and programs through online and face-to-face engagement, including the National Drought Network and the National Drought Forum.

National Drought Network

The National Drought Network (NDN) is an information sharing opportunity, hosted by the Australian, state and territory governments. It provides stakeholders with updates on drought related information on drought policy, programs and conditions.

Network meetings occur at least twice a year and can be scaled up if required. The next network meeting will be held in the second half of 2026.

The network is open to anyone who wishes to join. Content is suited to those from farming organisations, who work in or with the agricultural industry and those within farming communities – including not-for-profit organisations, charities, finance sector, unions and government sector.

If you are interested in joining, please email droughtpolicy@aff.gov.au

The fifth National Drought Network was held on Tuesday 28 October 2025 and attended virtually by over 170 people.

- Regional Investment Corporation concessional loans:

  - Announcement of additional $1 billion dollars for the Regional Investment Corporation, for concessional loans beyond 30 June 2026, helping farmers manage financial pressures during drought.

  - Announcement of Government response to the Review of the operation of the Regional Investment Corporation Act 2018 to be released this calendar year. The Government is currently reviewing RIC loan products to ensure they remain fit for purpose and provide meaningful support.

- Future Drought Fund (FDF) Programs:

  - $27 million Scaling Success Program: Applications opened on 17 October and will close on 4 December 2025. This is a targeted, invitation-only grant round, available exclusively to organisations that have successfully delivered projects under eligible past FDF programs.

  - $12 million Strengthening Drought Resilience on Country Program: Applications opened on 12 August and will close on 16 December 2025. This is a targeted grant round for First Nations-led organisations, with up to six Delivery Partners.

  - Upcoming virtual field day: Beanstalk’s Drought Venture Studio, with support from the FDF, is running a virtual field day on Thursday 20 November bringing together innovators with farmers to collaborate on ideas and share information.

- The FDF Drought Resilience and Adoption Hubs Review (the Hubs):

  - Release of the Future Drought Fund Drought Resilience Adoption and Innovation Hubs Review. The review, completed in March 2025, assessed performance, governance, and effectiveness, and sought community feedback to improve the program.

  - Discussion covered some of the key findings and recommendations of the report, including that the Hubs have strengthened relationships across diverse organisations to identify and deliver local priorities, but that improvements can be made.

  - The report includes 51 findings and 28 recommendations. The government will use these to inform a new competitive grant round, with an aim to open for applications by March 2026. Existing Hub arrangements will be extended for up to 12 months from July 2026 to ensure no gap in service during the transition.

- The 2025 National Drought Forum:

  - An overview of 2025 National Drought Forum was provided, including a summary of the key themes heard from stakeholders across the event. There was a survey run during this session that allowed participants at the NDN to provide further input and ideas via Slido.

- DAFF/ABA joint communications on financial support for drought:

  - This collaboration between DAFF and the Australian Banking Association will highlight how financial institutions can assist clients affected by drought conditions and emphasise that formal drought declarations are not required to access support. Launching on National Ag Day, Friday 21 November 2025.

- Farm Household Allowance and Farm Management Deposits Information sessions. As a result of feedback heard at the National Drought Forum 2025, information sessions were provided to increase stakeholder awareness of key features, benefits and eligibility criteria for

  - Farm Management Deposits (FMDs): FMDs are a risk management tool that aims to drive increased financial resilience and self-reliance. It is a tax incentive that encourages producers to build up cash reserves in their good years. The program creates a multi-million-dollar buffer for the agricultural industry. The FMD holdings total $6 billion across 40,500 accounts.

  - Farm Household Allowance (FHA): FHA is a structural adjustment program that provides a welfare safety net to give immediate hardship relief while the recipient works towards long term financial security.

The fourth meeting of the National Drought Network was held on Tuesday 11 February 2025, with over 120 people attending virtually.

- National Drought Network Terms of Reference - updated for 2025. This document sets out working arrangements for the network and guides how meeting and member information will be shared. The addition of the department’s privacy statement provides greater transparency on what the data collected will be used for.

- Future Drought Fund Investment Strategy - released 22 January 2025. This strategy is a roadmap of how programs will be funded and delivered during the current four-year funding cycle. The strategy responds to a recommendation made by the Productivity Commission in their review of the FDF in 2023 and to stakeholder feedback requesting fewer programs, longer and more impactful programs, guided by a clear plan.

- The Innovation Challenge Pilot - this is a new approach to finding innovative solutions to the many challenges arising from drought and climate change. The program is currently in the design phase. As part of this, DAFF is conducting a nationwide online consultation to gather insights from experts in drought and climate policy, thought leaders, community members, researchers, farmers, innovators and First Nations People. This feedback will inform the terms of reference and grant opportunity guidelines for the Innovation Challenges grant round to be launched in 2025. Consultation closes 13 March 2025 at 5pm (AEDT) and there is a link to the Have Your Say consultation here.

- FDF Drought Resilience Commercialisation Initiative - the Drought Venture Studio – delivered by Beanstalk Agtech – is now open for its third round of applications. Over the 2 year pilot, almost 100 innovators will have the opportunity to be part of the Drought Venture Studio and of these, 8 startups will be matched with an experienced Co-Founder for commercialisation and launch of their drought resilience product or service. Applications close on 9 March 2025 at 9pm AEDT.

- The Bureau of Meteorology provided updates on the current weather conditions and long-range forecast across Australia as well as the Bureau's climate and drought communications and tools. The Bureau highlighted the value of the Weekly Agriculture, Climate and Water Update. Subscribe by emailing agriculture@bom.gov.au.
