one account in the same industry with a financial institution, only one account is recorded in the published data. If that FMD holder also has an account in a different industry, one count for each industry is included in the published data. In 

addition, the value of deposits reported may be greater than the actual level of FMDs as primary producers may choose not to claim a tax deduction for all deposits held in FMD accounts.
