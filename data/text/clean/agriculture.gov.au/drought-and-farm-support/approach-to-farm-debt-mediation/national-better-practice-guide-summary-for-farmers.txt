Principle 3 – Consider support for personal issues, including mental and physical health 
Experiencing financial difficulty can be stressful and may lead to situational distress (a high level of 
stress arising from temporary circumstances) or  mental health issues. We encourage you to seek 
support and inform your lender of any circumstances that impact your ability to engage effectively.
