Cameron A, National Aquatic Animal Health Technical Working Group, 2004

The Australian and New Zealand standard diagnostic procedure (ANZSDP) for principles for the design

and conduct of surveys to show presence or absence of infectious disease in aquatic animals provides

standards and guidelines for implementing surveys for the purpose of demonstrating freedom from

aquatic animal diseases.
