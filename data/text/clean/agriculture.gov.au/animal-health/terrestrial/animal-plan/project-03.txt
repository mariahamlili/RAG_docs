Overview

This project developed robust whole genome sequencing procedures for the early detection and

characterisation of lumpy skin disease and African horse sickness viruses. The goal is to reduce the

consequences of their outbreaks in Australia.

The genomic sequencing probes and procedures are now available at the Australian Centre for Disease

Prevention. These serve as an invaluable resource in the event of a disease outbreak in Australia.
