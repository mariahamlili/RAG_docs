Overview

The Subcommittee on Animal Health Laboratory Standards, through leadership of the Department of

Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry and an expert consultant, have completed the process of finalising a

business plan. The plan looks towards a national laboratory-focused simulation exercise.

The exercise was completed in 2023. It helped identify ways to improve surge capacity of the Australian

animal health laboratory network in response to an emergency animal disease outbreak.
