Overview

This project will develop robust polymerase chain reaction (PCR) methods to detect Theileria equi and

Babesia Caballi, the pathogens that cause equine piroplasmosis (EP). These tests will complement

serological tests for EP at the Australian Centre for Disease Preparedness (ACDP).

The project will strengthen Australia's diagnostic capacity for EP. It will support testing needed for

trade-related certification in the equine industry. By establishing local, National Association of Testing

Authorities (NATA) accredited testing capability, the project will deliver faster, more reliable diagnosis

and reduce reliance on overseas laboratories. This will give greater confidence in Australia's equine

health standards.
