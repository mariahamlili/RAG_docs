Publication details

The Destruction Manual is an integral part of the Australian Aquatic Veterinary Emergency Plan

(AQUAVETPLAN).

Preventing the spread of disease might require the efficient and humane killing of stock. The manual

provides guiding principles on the decision to destroy stock and the choice and application of

appropriate techniques.
