Publication details

This disease strategy manual is an integral part of the Australian Aquatic Veterinary Emergency Plan

(AQUAVETPLAN).

The manual sets out the disease control principles for use in response to a suspected or confirmed

outbreak of abalone viral ganglioneuritis (AVG) in Australia.

AVG is a serious disease of abalone. It has had major impacts on wild abalone, farmed abalone and

abalone held in processing plants, causing high mortalities and substantial economic loss.
