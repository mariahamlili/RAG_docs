Australia is free from lumpy skin disease (LSD) and has never had an incursion of the disease.
LSD is a serious viral disease that primarily affects cattle and buffalo. The disease can result in animal
welfare issues and significant production losses. LSD is transmitted between animals. It is not a human
LSD is carried by live animals and reproductive material. It can also be spread by contaminated
equipment and biting insects such as flies and mosquitoes or parasites like ticks.
