If you notice sick or dead birds or other animals, you should not touch them. If there are dead birds or

other animals, take photos or a video.

Record your location and report it to the 24-hour Emergency Animal Disease Hotline on 1800 675 888.

The H5 strain (H5N1 clade 2.3.4.4b) has caused severe disease and high death rates in poultry, wild

birds and affected mammals.
