The Australia-United States Free Trade Agreement (AUSTFA) has 13 tariff rate quotas for exports to the United States. The Department provides management and administration of the tariff rate quotas under the Export Control (Tariff Rate Quota – General) Rules 2021 (the Rules).

The World Trade Organisation (WTO) has 4 tariff rate quotas for exports to the US. The Department only administers the US WTO supplementary TRQs volumes under the Export Control (Tariff Rate Quota – General) Rules 2021 (the Rules).
