To export goods as organic or bio-dynamic, you must meet all relevant export requirements. These requirements are set by Australian export laws and by the importing country.

New exporters — read our step-by-step guide to exporting organic or bio-dynamic goods.

Types of goods

These export requirements relate to any goods intended for export that are labelled or described, or ingredients that are labelled or described, as:

- organic

- biodynamic

- biological

- ecological

- other similar descriptions*

*This includes goods and/or ingredients that are described as ‘in conversion to organic or bio-dynamic’.

Find out more about Australian export laws for organic goods.

Importing country requirements

You must meet all conditions set by the importing country.

Prior to export:

- check our Manual of Importing Country Requirements (Micor)

- talk to the importing country authority

- check the organic export market access notices webpage.

Certified organic/bio-dynamic operators

You must use a certified organic/bio-dynamic operator to source, prepare and store your goods.

The organic/bio-dynamic operator must be certified. This certification must be issued by one of our approved certifying bodies.

You can also apply to become a certified organic/bio-dynamic operator.

Organic goods certificate

To export goods as organic or bio-dynamic, an organic goods certificate is required.

The organic goods certificate:

- ensures that Australian organic/bio-dynamic goods have been subject to a regulatory system that guarantees the organic/bio-dynamic production system underpinning the organic/bio-dynamic claims.

- provides assurance to the importing country that the goods have been produced in accordance with Australia’s organic export regulatory framework and importing country requirements,

- is a mandatory export certificate that is required for each consignment.

An organic goods certificate must be requested from an approved certifying body, prior to the export of the goods.

An organic goods certificate can only be issued after departure as a replacement certificate where there is already an existing certificate in place. Replacement certificates may only be issued to correct changes to export arrangements or product information.

It is your responsibility to ensure the details in your organic goods certificate are correct at the time of issue and match all other export certification.

Exceptions

Organic goods certificates may not be required for organic goods:

- exported to New Zealand.

- that are cosmetics.

- legally imported into Australia and exported with no further processing or repackaging.

You may also be exempt if you export organic goods:

- in a consignment whose total weight is less than 10kg in solid form or 10L in liquid form.

Where export documentation is an importing country requirement, this requirement must be met despite any exceptions.

Criminal and civil penalties may apply where a person contravenes prescribed export conditions.

Other export laws

You may also need to comply with other Australian export laws. These are separate to the requirements for organic or bio-dynamic goods.
