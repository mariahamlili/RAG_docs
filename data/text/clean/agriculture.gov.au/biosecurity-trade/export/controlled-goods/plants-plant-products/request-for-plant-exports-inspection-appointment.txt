Prescribed goods, empty containers and empty bulk vessels for export must be inspected by a plant

export Authorised Officer (AO). To arrange an inspection by a departmental AO, you must complete a

request for plant exports inspection appointment form.

Contact details as to where to submit the appointment form are provided in the Guidelines document.
