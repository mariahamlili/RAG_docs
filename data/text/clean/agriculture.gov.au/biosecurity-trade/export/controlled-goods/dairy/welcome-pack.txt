Hello and congratulations on starting your journey to become a dairy exporter.

We are your export facilitators and work at the department. I assess your dairy export application as well assist manufacturers like yourself in become registered to export dairy products. The link you have opened allows you to access our Welcome Pack.

The pack has been put together to introduce a range of topics we think are important for you to be aware of as you start your journey.

The topics include:

- Guide to becoming export registered

- Do you need export registration?

- Your journey map to becoming a registered exporter and

- Dairy export registration manual

And some other links that will be valuable to you.

This welcome pack has been designed as an introduction to the dairy export application process. It is not exhaustive, and we are sure you will have many more questions.

A good second step may be to focus on the dairy export registration manual. The information it contains is more detailed. I look forward to assisting you, on your journey to take your product to the international market.
