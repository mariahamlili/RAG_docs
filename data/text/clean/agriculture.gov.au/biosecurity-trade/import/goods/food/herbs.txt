Inspection and testing
This food is classified as a surveillance food.
We refer consignments for analytical testing at the rate of 5%. During inspection we will also conduct a
visual and label assessment.
Details on inspection and analytical testing can be found in the BICON food safety requirements case
for ‘Herbs and spices’.
