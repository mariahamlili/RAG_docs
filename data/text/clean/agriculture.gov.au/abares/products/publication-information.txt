All ABARES publications are released with a Digital Object Identifier (DOI)through a link to the metadata information about the publication. A DOI is a unique handle, taking the form of a character string, composed of a prefix and suffix which identifies the registrant and specific object associated with the DOI.

All ABARES publications are released with a Creative Commons licence to allow users free use of the content within the publication, subject to the conditions of the licence. The ABARES Creative Commons statement says:
