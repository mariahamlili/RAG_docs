ABARES produces economic research which provides a comprehensive account of historical trends in,

the outlook for, Australian fisheries, and specific reports into fisheries issues with economic impacts.

Information and analysis contained in these reports are accessed by policy makers, managers,

researchers and industry.

The Australian fisheries economic indicators report series presents detailed analysis of the

economic performance of selected Commonwealth fisheries.

Australian fisheries and aquaculture statistics contains a detailed historic analysis of fisheries,

production, consumption and trade.

The Annual Fisheries Outlook is a five-year forecast of the value and contribution of Australian

fisheries and aquaculture production to exports, and global and domestic seafood consumption.

It also includes specific forecasts for key species.

Source: https://www.agriculture.gov.au/abares/research-topics/fisheries/fisheries-economics
