Agricultural commodity statistics

Release update

The Agricultural Commodity Statistics report provides a set of comprehensive statistical tables with historical data on Australian and world prices, production, consumption, stocks and trade for Australia’s key rural commodities.

For ABARES most recent estimates and forecast data on Australian agricultural markets, see the Agricultural commodities and Australian crop reports.
