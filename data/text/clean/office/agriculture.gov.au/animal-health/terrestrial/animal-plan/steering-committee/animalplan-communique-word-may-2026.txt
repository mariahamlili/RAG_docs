Animalplan 2022 to 2027

Steering Committee Communiqué

The Animalplan Steering Committee combines government, industry and non-government organisation leaders to support projects and activities that strengthen Australia’s animal health system.

Key updates from the Animalplan Steering Committee and Secretariat

The Steering Committee met in May 2026 for the quarterly meeting. They discussed current and proposed Animalplan projects, the 2026 communications and engagement plan, and the committee’s terms of reference.

The Secretariat published project updates from the February to April 2026 quarter on the Animalplan website.

The next Animalplan Steering Committee meeting will be held in August 2026. The Secretariat will also then publish updates for the May to July 2026 quarter.

Animalplan will host its first webinar of the year on 7 August 2026. It will update stakeholders on progress against the National Animal Health Diagnostics Business Plan 2021-2026, including Exercise Waterhole.

More information will be shared closer to the event via DAFF social media and the Animalplan website.

Project news – February to April 2026

New projects:

#73: South Australian Red Meat and Wool Blueprint 2030

To establish a progressive and sustainable red meat and wool value chain in South Australia.

Updated projects:

#27: Emergency animal disease crisis management planning –

The project will end on 30 June 2026.

All crisis management plan templates and basic guidance videos are complete and a final workshop will be held in May 2026.

#44: Australia’s Animal Sector Antimicrobial Resistance Action Plan 2023 to 2028 –

Australia’s Animal Sector AMR Action Plan 2023-2028 is undergoing a mid-term review.

Risk based identification of 10 priority drug-bug-host combinations for surveillance is nearly complete.

Dairy Australia will soon finalise antimicrobial resistance testing to estimate susceptibility profiles for E. coli (Escherichia) and Enterococcus species in dairy cattle.

#64: Assessing the feasibility of testing trapped pooled insects for lumpy skin disease virus – Mosquitoes have been bred, raised and prepared for testing.

Pools of mosquitoes will be tested to understand the lowest level of contamination with lumpy skin disease virus that can be detected.

#66: Australian Animal Disease Genomics Initiative: building the molecular blueprints for animal health –  The Australian Animal Disease Genomics Initiative ran a second request for project partnership proposals to generate foundational genomic data for priority animal pathogens, parasites and viromes.

#67: Enhancing rapid decision-support for emergency animal disease outbreaks - HASTE – Phase one of the project has progressed to the final delivery and consolidation stage.

Core deliverables have been completed, including modelling workflows, advancements in TAPPAS-2 capability and delivery of two simulation workshops.

Engagement and collaboration with government and industry are ongoing to increase awareness and use of HASTE tools.

Final reporting is underway, with potential next-phase activities under consideration.

#72: CalfWays, Sustainable Dairy Calf Management Roadmap to 2035 – The CalfWays website is now live.

Organisations are encouraged to subscribe to the quarterly newsletter and explore opportunities to support or collaborate on CalfWays initiatives.
