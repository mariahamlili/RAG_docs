Anatomy

Finfish

The external and internal anatomy of finfish varies considerably across species. Specific adaptations to predator–prey interactions, aquatic habitat variability and dietary preferences can explain these differences. One example is the short intestinal length of carnivorous fish compared with the relatively long intestine of herbivorous fish. Another, is the ventrally directed mouth of bottom-feeding species such as European carp (Cyprinus carpio) compared with the upward oriented mouth of the surface-feeding saratoga (Scleropages leichardti).
