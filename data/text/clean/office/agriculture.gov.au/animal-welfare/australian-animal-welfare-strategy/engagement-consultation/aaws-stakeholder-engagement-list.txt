Australian Animal Welfare Strategy: stakeholder engagement list

This document lists the organisations invited to focus groups and other meetings. Individuals involved in the development of the original AAWS were also invited. Names of individuals are not listed.

This first round of consultation is one step in an extensive stakeholder engagement process that will take place over the next 3 years.
