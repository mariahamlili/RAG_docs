Renewed Australian 
Animal Welfare Strategy

The strategy will cement a national approach to animal welfare and signal to our international trading partners and Australian consumers that animal welfare is a priority for our nation.

It will also confirm Australia’s commitment to a modern, sustainable and science-based approach to animal welfare.

What’s in this year’s Budget

The government is strengthening our national approach to animal welfare by investing $5 million over 4 years to renew the Australian Animal Welfare Strategy, restoring much needed national leadership on our nation’s approach to animal welfare.

Why this is important

The strategy delivers on the government’s election commitment to strengthen animal welfare transparency and assurance, and complements the October Budget measure to create an Inspector-General of Animal Welfare and Live Animal Exports.

A renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy re-establishes a national framework for priority setting and associated national standards and measures of improvement for animal welfare outcomes.

Demonstrating Australia’s strong animal welfare standards will support increased access to overseas markets, while strengthening the reputation of our animal and agricultural industries with trading partners.

The renewal of the Australian Animal Welfare Strategy will be undertaken in collaboration with state and territory governments and in consultation with non-government and industry bodies, animal welfare groups, First Nations peoples and the Australian community.
