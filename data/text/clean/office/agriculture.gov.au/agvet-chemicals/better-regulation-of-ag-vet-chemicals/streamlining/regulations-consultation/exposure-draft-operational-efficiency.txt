Part 5—Application, saving and transitional provisions
5.1  Transitional matters relating to the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Legislation Amendment (Operational Efficiency) Regulations 2018
Savings—Exemptions from old annual return requirements
	(1)	Despite the repeal of regulation 4.10 by the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Legislation Amendment (Operational Efficiency) Regulations 2018 (the amending regulations), that regulation, as in force immediately before the commencement of this item, continues to apply:
	(a)	in respect of the matters mentioned in subitem 8(1) of Schedule 1 to the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Legislation Amendment (Operational Efficiency) Act 2018 (the amending Act); and
	(b)	in relation to the 2017‑18 financial year or an earlier financial year.
Savings—Infringement notices for contravening old annual return requirements
	(2)	Despite the repeal of table item 8 of the table in Schedule 5 by the amending regulations, that table item, as in force immediately before the commencement of this item, continues to apply on and after that commencement in respect of a contravention of subsection 69E(1) of the Act, as applied by subitem 8(1) of Schedule 1 to the amending Act.
