Detailed response to APVMA reviews implementation update

September 2025 to March 2026

Key highlights

The department and the APVMA continue to implement reforms to address the recommendations since the release of the Detailed response to the final report on future structure and governance arrangements for the Australian Pesticides and Veterinary Medicines Authority on 4 November 2024. Activities that have been implemented this reporting period include the publication of the APVMA’s Regulatory Posture Statement 2026–30, while the department continues to prioritise work towards a sustainable funding model for the APVMA as a key enabling reform.
