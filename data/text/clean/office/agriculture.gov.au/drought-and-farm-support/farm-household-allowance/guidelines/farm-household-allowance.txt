Farm Household Allowance

Training Providers and the $10,000 Activity Supplement

Training providers

If you have a specialised skill and train others with your expertise, FHA recipients may want to access your training.

People who receive FHA have a $10,000 Activity Supplement available to help develop skills, access training and pay for advice to improve their long-term financial position. Thousands of people have already accessed the Activity Supplement with tens of millions of dollars paid. There is a diverse range of training activities to help them achieve financial self-reliance.

FHA can support recipients to undertake your training. If approved, your fee could be paid directly or you could receive upfront payment from a participant who will be reimbursed.

Businesses that are not a Registered Training Organisation

If you are a training provider who is not a Registered Training Organisation (RTO), you can still take part if you meet a couple of key requirements.

You are also helping to upskill rural Australia. Aside from business and farm-based training, approvals have been given for a diverse range of activities that can help generate income on-farm or off-farm.

If the activity is not approved for reimbursement, the participant can still undertake the training at their own expense.

Assessment process

Each Non-RTO request is assessed on its own merits – there is no hard pass / fail. The training offered by a Non-RTO can be niche, unique or mainstream.

There are a few things we need:

Proposed learning outcomes

Business insurance

We will also ask you about:

Qualification/s of the trainer/s

Experience in delivering training

Membership of professional / industry organisations

Once you’ve provided this information you won’t need to supply it again if more FHA recipients are interested in your training, unless your insurance needs updating or your trainers change.

More information

If you are a Non-RTO provider and have any questions about eligibility, or want clarification, please email fha@aff.gov.au

For any other enquiries about the FHA program, call the Farmer Assistance Hotline on 132 316 from 8am to 5pm each weekday.

The $10,000 Activity Supplement

The $10,000 Activity Supplement available to FHA recipients is taxpayer funding to help develop skills, access training or receive professional advice with a view to assist farming families to improve their financial circumstances.

Previous examples of training include:

Business and accounting courses

Computer and web design courses

Leadership and strategy courses

Pasture and grazing management training

Marketing courses

Risk management training

Animal husbandry training

Chemical and food safety certification

Regenerative land management training

Natural resource management courses

Pilot licence training

Heavy vehicle training

Yoga and massage courses

Aerial drone training

This is not an exhaustive list but it does highlight a diverse range of training approvals.
