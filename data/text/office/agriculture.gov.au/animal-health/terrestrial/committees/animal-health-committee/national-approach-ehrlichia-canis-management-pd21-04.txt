August 2021

Policy Decision 21-04

National approach to Ehrlichia canis (E. canis) management

Animal Health Committee

Recommendations

Nationally co-ordinated public information campaign promoting:

Awareness of E. canis and management of the vector

Guidelines for dog owners and breeders with dogs travelling or moving into/through areas where E. canis is known or likely to be active or where the vector is known to be present

Guidelines (promoted as protective biosecurity measures) for dog owners, breeders and rehoming organisations with dogs travelling or moving out of areas where E. canis is known or likely to be active to areas where E. canis is not established

Messaging should be simple and in language appropriate to meet stakeholder communication needs.

National guidelines for veterinarians investigating and managing cases of E. canis, including information for dog owners on long-term management of E. canis.

Additional recommendations where ‘infection with E. canis’ is listed as a nationally notifiable disease or notifiable under jurisdictional legislation:

Passive surveillance program in areas where E. canis is not known to be active

Active exclusion testing for suspect cases in areas where E. canis is not active or known to be active

National data management and reporting for E. canis surveillance through NAHIS.
