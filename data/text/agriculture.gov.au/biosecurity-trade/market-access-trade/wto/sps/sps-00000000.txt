Australian Government Department of Agriculture Fisheries and Forestry and AusAID, 2005
This booklet outlines the basic concepts of the World Trade Organization (WTO) Agreement on the
Application of Sanitary and Phytosanitary Measures (SPS Agreement). All WTO member countries are
bound by the SPS Agreement. This booklet is important for anyone interested in international trade in
agricultural commodities and was funded by the former AusAID under the Sanitary and Phytosanitary
Capacity Building Program managed by the Office of the Chief Plant Protection Officer.
