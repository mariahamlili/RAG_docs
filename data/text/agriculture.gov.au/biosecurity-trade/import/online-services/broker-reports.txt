Broker Reports has been decommissioned and is no longer available.
For more than 20 years, it provided brokers and importers with access to reporting on their AIMS entries, including invoicing status and entry-level data.
After supporting industry for over two decades, the service has now been retired as part of the department’s transition to modern digital services.
Replacement service
These reporting capabilities are now available through Biosecurity Industry Reporting in the Biosecurity Portal.
Biosecurity Industry Reporting provides approved arrangement class 19 biosecurity industry participants and accredited persons with visibility of their in-progress entries referred to the department. The reports provide users with consignment-specific and historical data and enable a search capability for a specific entry reference to find details on:
- Cargo status
- Fee reporting
- Approved arrangement class 19.2 usage and uptake
This service replaces the information previously available through Broker Reports and forms part of the department’s ongoing improvements to industry-facing digital services.
