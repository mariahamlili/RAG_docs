Reduced risk classification
Offshore manufacturers of inorganic fertiliser which meet the department’s eligibility and application requirements may be approved to hold a reduced risk classification with the department. The requirements to apply for a reduced risk classification are outlined in the inorganic fertiliser policy documents.
Consignments of inorganic fertiliser originating from a manufacturer that holds a reduced risk classification that also meet the relevant importing conditions set by the department will be subject to reduced biosecurity intervention on arrival.

Fertiliser key
ASN – Ammonium sulphate nitrate
CAN - Calcium ammonium nitrate
DAP - Di-ammonium phosphate
DSP – Double super phosphate
MAP - Mono-ammonium phosphate
MicroEssentials – Nitrogen phosphorus pentoxide sulphur compounds
MOP - Muriate of potash
NK – Nitrogen potassium compound
NP - Nitrogen phosphorous compound
NPK - Nitrogen phosphorous potassium
NPS - Nitrogen phosphorous sulphur
SOP - Sulphate of potash
SSP - Single super phosphate
TSP (SP36) - Triple super phosphate
Note: The above list is not exhaustive and does not contain the details of all holders of reduced risk classifications. The department only publishes the details of holders of reduced risk classifications who have provided publication consent.
