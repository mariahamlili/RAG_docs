Department of Agriculture, Water and the Environment, September 2020
The department must be notified of imported animal transport equipment through the submission of a Movement advice for animal transport equipment.
This includes:
- equipment transiting through Australia
- equipment accompanying the import of an animal
- empty equipment to be used domestically or for export of live animals.
This advice must be completed by the airline, agent/customs broker or the importer of the equipment and submitted to the department at the Australian destination port, prior to arrival of equipment in Australia.
Contact details to be used for notification are located on the advice.
