Publication details
Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry, 2025
Complete the Application for the transfer or release of biosecurity material form to apply for a permit
to:
transfer biological material currently held in an approved arrangement (AA) site to another AA
site or multiple AA sites.
release biological material from an AA site (treatment may be required before release – this will
be specified on the release permit and will require an assessment of the treatment to ensure
biosecurity risks are sufficiently addressed).
Paying for your application
Before your application is processed by the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry, you
must pay the prescribed fee in full. Please refer to the department’s charging guidelines for details of
fees and charges.
Manual applications can be paid for using the credit card payment form.
To apply please submit the below application form along with a completed credit card payment form
to imports@aff.gov.au.
