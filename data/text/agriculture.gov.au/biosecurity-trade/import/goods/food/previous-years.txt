Holding orders for failing food
We apply a holding order for imported food:

We ensure future consignments of this food are inspected under the Imported Food Inspection Scheme
(IFIS).
Holding orders apply until the basis for the holding order no longer applies or when there have been 5
consecutive passes demonstrating a history of compliance.
