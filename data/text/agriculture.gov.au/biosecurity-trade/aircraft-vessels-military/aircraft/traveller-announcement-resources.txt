Access our approved incoming traveller announcements for use on all international flights. They are available in several languages.
Incoming traveller announcements must NOT be edited.
They provide important information on Australia’s biosecurity requirements.
Incoming traveller announcement
The incoming traveller announcement is a legal requirement on all international flights and must be delivered before the first landing place in Australian territory.
Aircraft operators are strongly encouraged to deliver the announcement close to top of descent.
On-arrival medical announcement
Border officials may need to board an aircraft to attend to an ill traveller, or a traveller who has died in-flight.
In this event, an announcement may be aired on-arrival.
