Publication details
External Territories Team, November 2019
The purpose of the external Territory – Norfolk Island web pages is to convey recent amendments made to the Biosecurity Legislation (Prohibited and Conditionally Non-prohibited Goods) Determination 2016. The content of these pages provides/communicates the latest import requirements, specifically the health certification requirements, for cats and dogs being imported into Norfolk Island. These pages will be utilised by the Norfolk Island community and industry.
