Stakeholder submissions on the Review of the biosecurity risks of prawns imported from all countries for human consumption – draft report, are published below. We received 17 submissions and have been given permission to publish 12. These submissions were made in response to Biosecurity Advice 2020-05. This consultation forms part of the Review of the biosecurity risks for prawns.
All submissions are being considered as we prepare the final report.
