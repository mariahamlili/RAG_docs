Steps to complete this risk analysis
When we do a risk analysis, we:
- start the risk analysis by reviewing the science on pests and diseases of concern
- assess and analyse biosecurity risks
- develop proposed risk management measures, if required
- consult the public on the draft report and then review comments
- publish a final report
- develop import conditions
- publish import conditions in our Biosecurity Import Conditions System (BICON)
This analysis was completed in 2021. Importers will soon be able to apply for a permit via BICON. Register in BICON to receive an alert when we publish the import conditions.

About the final report
We recommend that the importation of zoo bovids be allowed from approved countries. All imports must meet the agreed biosecurity import conditions.
The final report details:
- diseases of biosecurity concern for this commodity
- risk assessments for the identified diseases of biosecurity concern
We identified twenty-six diseases in the hazard analysis for assessment. The report makes recommendations on risk management measures for 22 diseases where the risk is not managed by general zoo import conditions.
