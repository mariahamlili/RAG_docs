The Biosecurity Training Centre (BTC) has transitioned to the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF) Academy and will be known as the DAFF Academy Regulatory Faculty.
The DAFF Academy Regulatory Faculty supports regulatory training and development for our employees. It builds on the regulatory training previously delivered by the Biosecurity Training Centre.
The Regulatory Faculty supports:
- regulatory and scientific capability development
- statutory training obligations and authorisation requirements
- industry and international biosecurity capability uplift.
Training and enquiries
Although training is primarily delivered to our employees, we will consider requests from external organisations and government agencies. Training is not offered to individual members of the public.
