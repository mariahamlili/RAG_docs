Biosecurity helps protect Australia’s plants and animals from the impact of invasive and exotic pests, weeds and diseases. It’s a big job and we need everyone to be involved.
These classroom resources have been prepared to help you teach your students about biosecurity and how we can all care for Country by keeping a Top Watch!

Other classroom resources
Top Watch! Tag
Top Watch! Tag is a game of chasey with a twist. Your students will take turns playing junior biosecurity officers trying to prevent pests and diseases getting past our biosecurity officers. To play you will need a set of instructions and tags. Email topwatch@aff.gov.au to order your set of tags.
If you have trouble accessing this file please contact topwatch@aff.gov.au.
