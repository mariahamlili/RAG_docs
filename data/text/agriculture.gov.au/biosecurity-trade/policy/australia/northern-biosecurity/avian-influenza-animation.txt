What you can do
- Keep a TopWatch! for avian influenza in your community.
- Avoid contact with sick or dead birds.
- If you see sick or dead birds, report it to a ranger, biosecurity officer, government vet, or call the Emergency Animal Disease Hotline on 1800 675 888.
Avian influenza (or bird flu) is a highly contagious disease that affects several species, mainly birds.
