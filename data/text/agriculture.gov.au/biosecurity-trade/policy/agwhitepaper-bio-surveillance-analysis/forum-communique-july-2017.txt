Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, July 2017
On 13 July 2017, more than 40 stakeholders attended the department’s Agricultural Competitiveness White Paper (Biosecurity) Forum at the Hyatt Hotel, Canberra. Departmental subject matter experts discussed with stakeholders the projects under four biosecurity themes within the White Paper. More details on what was discussed and the key points that emerged on the day, are contained in the Communique.
