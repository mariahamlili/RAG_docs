On 28 February 2024, the Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry announced that the Government is extending its commitment to transparency and accountability with the creation of a new Sustainable Biosecurity Funding Advisory Panel (the Panel). Details of the announcement are available in the Minister’s media release.
The Panel has been created to provide increased transparency for key contributors of biosecurity funding including producers and importers, and will build closer collaboration between government and key stakeholders, with regular meetings to share insights, discuss biosecurity priorities and explain spending decisions.
Panel members will be provided information about what revenue is being raised from producers and importers, how that money is being invested, in addition to updates on the rollout of the Sustainable Biosecurity Funding package measures.
The role of the Sustainable Biosecurity Funding Advisory Panel is to provide transparency of biosecurity expenditure and give biosecurity stakeholders – including producers and importers – genuine input into biosecurity priority setting for Australia’s biosecurity system.
The first meeting was held on 30 April 2024.
Panel membership
The Deputy Secretary Biosecurity, Operations and Compliance Group of the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry chairs the Panel.
Membership of the Panel is drawn from major agriculture, fisheries and forestry sectors, importers, environmental and research organisations.
