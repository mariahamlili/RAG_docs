The National Biosecurity Emergency Preparedness Expert Group was established by the National Biosecurity Committee (NBC) in 2015 to enhance Australia’s biosecurity emergency preparedness, response and initial recovery arrangements. The expert group reported directly to the NBC. NBEPEG was abolished In March 2020.
Membership and Chair
The expert group’s membership was comprised of emergency response specialists from each jurisdiction, Animal Health Australia and Plant Health Australia.
It was chaired by an NBC member.
Secretariat support for the expert group was provided by the Department of Agriculture.
