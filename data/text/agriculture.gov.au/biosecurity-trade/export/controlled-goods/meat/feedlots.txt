Feedlots must be EUCAS accredited if they want to produce animals that can be exported as meat to the
EU. A feedlot is a registered business covered by a single Property Identification Code (PIC) under State
or Territory legislation where cattle are confined and fed high–energy rations to maximise their growth
prior to slaughter. Feedlots may contain segregated EUCAS and non–EUCAS cattle.
