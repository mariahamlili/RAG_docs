Meat and meat products will transition from EXDOC to NEXDOC on 6 July 2026. Following this transition, weekly meat export statistics and end-of-month red meat export reports will be produced from the updated source system and you may notice some minor changes to the appearance or data in the Australian red meat export statistics reports. 
For more information about the NEXDOC transition, visit Transitioning commodities to NEXDOC. If you have questions about the published data, please contact quota.admin@aff.gov.au.
The Department's Quota Unit publishes monthly statistics on red meat exports from Australia to more than 57 destination markets. See also Quota usage reports.
