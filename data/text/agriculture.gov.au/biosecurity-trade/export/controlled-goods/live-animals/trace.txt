What is TRACE?
TRACE is our online service for managing livestock export approvals and Notices of Intention (NOIs).
We also use it to manage regulatory information about:
livestock export licences
registered establishments
accredited veterinarians.
Notices of Intention
If you are a licensed livestock exporter or export animal reproductive material, you need to use TRACE
to:
submit an NOI
view export licences (livestock exporters).
Set up TRACE in the Export Service
Access to TRACE is in the Export Service. To submit or manage Notices of Intention (NOIs), you will
need to create an account.
Get set up in the Export Service
Follow our step-by-step guide TRACE onboarding to the Export Service.
After your account is set up, you need to connect TRACE to the Export Service.
If you can’t access the Export Service to submit your NOI, email us at:
TRACE@aff.gov.au for livestock exports
genetics@aff.gov.au for animal reproductive material exports.
Contact us
You can contact us at one of our live animal export offices.
