Export permit issuer declaration of understanding
If you’re an exporter, operator of registered establishments or their employee and agent use this form to
apply to be an automated export permit issuer (AEPI). The form declares eligibility and demonstrate
understanding of export eligibility requirements. Submit this form when you apply for dairy export
registration application.
