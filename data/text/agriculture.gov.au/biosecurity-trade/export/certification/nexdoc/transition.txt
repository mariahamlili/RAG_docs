Most inedible meat export certification is now issued through NEXDOC.
Inedible meat products include raw and processed pet food, rendered products, and other animal-derived materials.
Exporters, agents, and freight forwarders are now required to submit certification requests through NEXDOC. While most certification processes have moved to the platform, some manual documents remain in use.
To clarify which documents are still issued manually, please contact nexdoc@aff.gov.au.
All certificate applications — including the NX188B, NX188M, and NX418 (formerly EX188B, EX188M, and M418) — have transitioned to the NEXDOC system.
