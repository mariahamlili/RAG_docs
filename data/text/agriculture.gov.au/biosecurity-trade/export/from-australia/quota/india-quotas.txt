The Australia-India Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (AI-ECTA) has 5 tariff rate quotas for
exports to India. The Department provides management and administration of the tariff rate quotas
under the Export Control (Tariff Rate Quota – General) Rules 2021 (the Rules).
