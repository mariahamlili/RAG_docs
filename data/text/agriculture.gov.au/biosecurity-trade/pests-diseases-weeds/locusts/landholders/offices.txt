Location of APLC bases
The APLC has staff located in Canberra and field bases in Narromine, Broken Hill and Longreach.
The APLC is headed by a Director who is accountable to the Commissioners. APLC staff are employed through the Australia Government Department of Agriculture and Water Resources.
