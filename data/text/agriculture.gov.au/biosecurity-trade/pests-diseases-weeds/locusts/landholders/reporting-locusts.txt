It is important that any locust activity is reported as soon as possible to your state biosecurity agency, or
department of primary industries or agriculture.
You can also report locust activity directly to the Australian Plague Locust Commission (APLC) by
phoning 1800 635 962 (an answering machine is provided for after-hours calls), or by completing the
online form below. Alternatively, you can complete a hard copy of this form PDF  [186 KB, 1 page] and
fax it to the APLC on 02 6272 5074.
