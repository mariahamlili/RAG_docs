Department of Agriculture, Water and the Environment, March 2020
The Australian Ballast Water Management Requirements provide guidance on how vessel operators
should manage ballast water when operating within Australian seas in order to comply with the
Biosecurity Act 2015. They also align to the International Convention for the Control and Management
of Ships’ Ballast Water and Sediments 2004 (the Ballast Water Management Convention), which
entered into force internationally on 8 September 2017.
