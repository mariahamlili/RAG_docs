Cost recovery export arrangement financial results 2024-25 reports outline the financial performance for 7 agricultural export arrangements. Each report is complimentary to the Cost Recovery Implementation Statement (CRIS). The reports include information on the financial and non-financial performance of each export arrangement as outlined in the Australian Government Cost Recovery Policy.
