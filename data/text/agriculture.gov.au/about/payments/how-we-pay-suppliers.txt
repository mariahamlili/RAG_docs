eInvoicing
This page contains information for suppliers invoicing the department for goods and or services rendered and the department’s preferred invoice transmission method, eInvoicing.
eInvoicing is the digital transmission of standardised invoice information between suppliers' and buyers' software through a secure network. It is a more efficient, accurate and secure way of invoicing rather than sending and receiving invoices as PDFs or other files via email.
Peppol
The Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry is registered and can receive eInvoices via Peppol. Our Peppol ID is: 0151:34190894983 which can also be found on the Peppol Directory.
For more information on eInvoicing, please visit eInvoicing (Australian Taxation Office).
