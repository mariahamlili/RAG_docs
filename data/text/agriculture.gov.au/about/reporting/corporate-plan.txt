Corporate Plan 2026–27
Our corporate plan is our primary planning document for the next 4 financial years. We use it to set our goals and explain how we will measure our performance. The corporate plan explains who we are, what we do and the environment that we operate in. It describes the key activities we undertake to deliver our purpose and how we work with others.
We review and update the corporate plan annually to meet the requirements of the Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013.
We report on our performance each year in our annual report.
