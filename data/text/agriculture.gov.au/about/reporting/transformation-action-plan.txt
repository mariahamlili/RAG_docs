Our Transformation Action Plan identifies the actions we will take to uplift our leadership and culture, collaboration, delivery, people and resourcing and risk capabilities.
Our goal through this plan is to be a proactive leader to deliver a more sustainable and prosperous Australia through agricultural production, trade, and biosecurity.
Priority actions
We have set 10 priority actions:
- Enhancing our Governance System
- Enterprise Performance and Reporting
- Workforce Planning and Development
- Financial Management
- Strategic Policy
- 10-year Digital Sustainment Investment Roadmap
- Embed an Enterprise Approach to Program Evaluation
- Stakeholder Collaboration and Practice Framework
- Improving Regulatory Service Delivery
- Information and Data Maturity Roadmap
Each action is detailed in our Transformation Action Plan.
Capability review
The Transformation Action Plan was developed in response to a Capability Review of the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry. This review was undertaken between May and August 2023 as part of the Australian Government’s broader APS reform agenda.
The report identified areas for capability improvement across the 5 domains of the capability review framework:
- leadership and culture
- collaboration
- delivery
- people
- resourcing and risk.
See the capability review report (Australian Public Service Commission website).
