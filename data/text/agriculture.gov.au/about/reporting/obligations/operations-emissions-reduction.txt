The Australian Government released the Net Zero in Government Operations Strategy in November 2023. It sets out the overall approach and action required by Commonwealth entities to achieve the APS Net Zero 2030 target.
The Department of Agriculture Fisheries and Forestry is committed to the achievement of the Australian Government’s APS Net Zero 2030 target. The department is following the APS Net Zero 2030 target in full, as per the Net Zero in Government Operations Strategy.
Operations Emissions Reduction Plan 2024-2030
This Operations Emissions Reduction Plan describes the priorities and actions the department is taking to reduce operational emissions and contribute to the APS Net Zero 2030 target.
