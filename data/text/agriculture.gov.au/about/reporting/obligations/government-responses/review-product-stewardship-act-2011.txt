About this response
The findings of the Review of the Product Stewardship Act 2011 are welcomed, and the department has commenced actions to implement the recommendations.
The recommendations cover three broad themes:
- overall effectiveness of the Act
- product stewardship initiatives under the Act
- the National Television and Computer Recycling Scheme.
