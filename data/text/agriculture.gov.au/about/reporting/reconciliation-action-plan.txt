Our Vision for Reconciliation
DAFF is committed to taking practical actions to achieve reconciliation through our Reconciliation Action Plan 2021 to 2024 (RAP).
We focus on strengthening connections to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and learn what is important to them.
Our RAP identifies how we will support, build and improve relationships with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples both in our department and their communities. It articulates our shared determination to advance reconciliation in our workplaces and communities and sets a path for us to achieve our vision through a practical plan of action.
Our RAP represents our vision to:
- attract and retain more Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to our workforce
- shape our business practices
- ensure our people are culturally competent and engaged
- develop activities that are appropriate in the journey to reconciliation.
