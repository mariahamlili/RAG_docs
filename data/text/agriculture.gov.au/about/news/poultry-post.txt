Poultry Post newsletter is for domestic and commercial bird owners and poultry producers in Australia. This includes people who keep birds as a hobby, have a couple of backyard chooks or run a small free-range flock.
This newsletter provides:
- timely updates on threats to birds such as H5 avian influenza (bird flu)
- biosecurity alerts and outbreak notifications
- practical advice to help you keep your birds safe and healthy.
Biosecurity is everyone’s responsibility. With ongoing global challenges, staying informed is the best way to safeguard your birds.
