If you’re a farmer dealing with drought, you don’t have to do it alone.
There is support available to help you navigate tough times in your region, including:
- Free, independent rural financial counselling
- Farm Household Allowance
- Low interest loans
- Draw downs on Farm Management Deposits, and
- Mental health and wellbeing services to support you and your family
These programs are available now and they’re not tied to drought declarations.
So don’t wait to know your options. See if you’re eligible and take the first step towards support.
Learn more at drought.gov.au.
