Are you prepared for the next drought?
There are steps and tools available to help you prepare ahead of time – so you’re ready when it’s needed most.
They include:
- Farm business planning and coaching
- Climate information
- Low interest RIC loans
- Farm Management Deposits
- Tax concessions for farm fencing, dams, pumps, fodder sheds, and more.
Being proactive today could make all the difference.
Don’t get caught out by the next drought.
Learn more at drought.gov.au.
