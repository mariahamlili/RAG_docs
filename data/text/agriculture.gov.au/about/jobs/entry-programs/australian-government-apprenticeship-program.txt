About the Program
The Australian Government Apprenticeship Program (AGAP) is a flagship initiative of the Australian Public Service.
It provides individuals from diverse backgrounds including school leavers, recent graduates, and those seeking a career change with a pathway to a rewarding career.
Recruitment for the AGAP is managed by the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations.
Through the AGAP Program, you’ll gain firsthand experience working within government, explore a variety of roles, and contribute to meaningful projects that make a real impact on the lives of Australians. AGAP is a pathway to professional growth, valuable networking, and the chance to make a genuine difference.
At the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF) we work to:
- Protect Australia’s natural resources
- Develop strong agricultural industries
- Deliver policies and programs for a strong economy.
Your Role
The Australian Government Apprenticeship Program AGAP offers a structured 12-month learning and development experience designed to support your professional growth.
During the program, you will:
- Receive a paid salary at the APS3 classification
- Gain hands-on experience aligned with our department’s key priorities
- Collaborate on engaging projects alongside professionals and researchers in your area of interest
- Advance to the APS4 classification upon successful completion of the program.
As a participant, you will help us achieve our objectives, including:
Biosecurity: Enhancing Australia’s biosecurity system to protect our people, environment, and economy from current and emerging threats.
Resilience and Sustainability: Supporting agriculture, forestry, and fisheries to contribute to a healthy, sustainable, and low-emissions future.
Industry Growth: Helping drive progress toward a $100 billion agriculture, fisheries, and forestry industry by 2030 in a dynamic global market.
Benefits
Benefits of the AGAP program include:
Comprehensive Support and Networking: Benefit from a dedicated support team, and access to the department’s entry programs network.
Dynamic and Collaborative: Engage as a valued member of a team in a dynamic and collaborative environment, while gaining valuable experience working alongside and learning from experts in their respective fields.
Competitive Benefits and Flexibility: Enjoy a competitive remuneration package, relocation assistance, and flexible work arrangements, ensuring a balanced and rewarding work-life experience.
For more information on the department's workplace conditions, refer to the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry Enterprise Agreement.

Eligibility
- Be an Australian Citizen
- Undergo a police check
- Obtain and maintain a valid Australian Government security clearance
- Be prepared to study a comprehensive Learning and Development Program.
