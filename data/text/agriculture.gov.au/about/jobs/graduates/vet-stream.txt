About the stream
Join one of Australia’s largest employers of vets and kickstart your public service career with real impact. Our 12-month Graduate Development Program is your chance to grow your skills, make a difference, and help protect Australia’s animals and agriculture.
What you’ll do
As a veterinary graduate, you’ll:
- Help shape policies on animal health, diseases, and welfare.
- Support safe trade by developing science-based import/export standards.
- Work on emergency response plans for animal disease outbreaks.
- Contribute to animal welfare strategies for livestock at home and overseas.
Why join us?
- Diverse career paths: Gain hands-on experience in biosecurity, exports, and welfare.
- Supportive environment: Access mentoring, training, and a strong graduate community.
- Great benefits: Enjoy competitive pay, relocation help, and flexible work options.
- Real-world learning: Rotate through different teams, from policy to operations.
- Professional growth: Join APS-wide graduate events, get formal training, and build your network.
