Numann ID, Jusoh MMd and Lumb E, 2003
A booklet published by the Office of the Chief Plant Protection Officer in the then Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry to highlight scientific invertebrate collections maintained in the South-East Asian region that include preserved specimens of insects and their relatives, especially mites, spiders, millipedes, centipedes and terrestrial crustaceans.
