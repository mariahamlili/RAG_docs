Overview
Animal Health Australia (AHA) developed the National Biosecurity Manual for the Ratite Industry. It was funded by AgriFutures and in collaboration with ratite industries.
The manual was published in September 2023.
Project Lead: AHA
Project Contact: aha@animalhealthaustralia.com.au
Project Status: Complete
Objective alignment:
- 3 – Improve the adoption and implementation of biosecurity practices throughout the terrestrial animal industry supply chain.
Activity alignment:
- 3.4 – Continue developing biosecurity guidelines for the supply chains of novel small-scale industries.
