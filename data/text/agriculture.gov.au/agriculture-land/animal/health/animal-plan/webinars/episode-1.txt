Animalplan launch
On 1 March 2023, we hosted a webinar to celebrate the launch of Animalplan.
The webinar outlined projects that align to these Animalplan objectives:
- Improving Australia’s preparedness and ability to respond to emergency animal diseases.
- Improving Australia’s surveillance and diagnostic capacity for animal pests and diseases.
- Managing the risk of antimicrobial resistance.
- Implementing industry sustainability frameworks and plans.
- Improving the integrity of animal health systems.
Hosts: Dr Narelle Clegg, Assistant Secretary, Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF) and Dr Kylie Hewson, Chair of the Animalplan Steering Committee
Presenters:
- Olivia Liu, Aquatic Pest and Health Policy, DAFF, on AQUAPLAN.
- Dr Graeme Cooke, Deputy Chair of the Animalplan Steering Committee, Chief Veterinary Officer, Victorian Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA), on Animalplan 2022 to 2027.
- Dr Sam Hamilton, Director of the Animal Strategy and Coordination Branch, DAFF, on the National Lumpy Skin Disease Action Plan.
- Dr Christine Morton, Animal Health Policy Branch, DAFF, on Animalplan 2022 to 2027, surveillance and diagnostics.
- Dr Jo Coombe, Secretariat for the Animal Industries’ Antimicrobial Stewardship Research, Development and Extension Strategy, project update on mitigating on-farm antimicrobial resistance risks for livestock industries.
- Mr Warwick Ragg, General Manager, Natural Resource Management, National Farmers Federation, on the Australian Agriculture Sustainability Framework.
- Joanna Bunting, Assistant Secretary of the Agricultural Traceability Branch, DAFF, on agricultural traceability and the integrity of animal health systems.
