Overview
The desktop and functional exercises were completed with great success in 2023.
- Activities 1 and 2 were conducted at AgriBio, Bundoora, Victoria in September 2023.
  - The focus of these 2 desktop activities were laboratory resources and logistics.
- Activity 3 was conducted at the Tasmanian Animal Health Laboratory in October 2023.
  - The focus of this exercise was to evaluate Tasmania’s animal health laboratory systems and prepare for Activity 4.
- Activity 4 was conducted across all Australian state/territory government animal health laboratories, including the Australian Centre for Disease Preparedness, in November 2023.
  - The focus of this 3-day activity was to challenge each laboratory with a simulated disease outbreak.
Project Lead: Sub Committee on Animal Health Laboratory Standards, Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry
Project Contact: animalhealthlaboratories@aff.gov.au
Project Status: Complete
Project date: September to November 2023
Objective alignment:
- 2 – Improve Australia’s surveillance and diagnostic capacity for animal pests and diseases.
Activity alignment:
- 2.1 – Implement actions under National Animal Health Surveillance Business Plan and National Animal Health Diagnostic Business Plan.
