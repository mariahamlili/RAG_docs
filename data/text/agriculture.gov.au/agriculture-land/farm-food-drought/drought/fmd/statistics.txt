FMD statistics—detailed state/territory and industry totals
Financial institutions offering FMDs are required to report details of the FMDs they hold at the end of a month to the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry. Reports must be submitted by close of business on the 10th calendar day of the following month. The department endeavours to release a summary of these statistics by the 15th calendar day of each month. The department cannot prepare a summary of the statistics until it has received returns from all relevant financial institutions.
Please Note:
- From July 2020, the state/territory industry FMD accounts and holdings have been aggregated with the respective New South Wales industry FMD accounts and holdings where there are less than 20 accounts for privacy reasons, previously it was less than 5.
- If you have undertaken any analyses on the original statistics, please be aware that these corrections may alter your calculations and therefore require amendments.
- This excel spreadsheet shows the variations between the statistics originally published and the corrected statistics now available.
- If you would like more in-depth information about the revisions that were made to the returns from June 2017 to February 2019, please email FMD Returns.
