The Future Drought Fund (FDF) helps build drought and climate resilience across Australia.
These case studies tell the stories of the farmers, land managers and communities putting FDF programs into action.
Explore the case studies using the interactive map or browse through the list. The map also highlights the 8 national Drought Resilience Adoption and Innovation Hubs (the hubs). The hubs foster supportive relationships to identify and deliver on local priorities. They play an important role to deliver FDF projects, build resilience, and share knowledge across the fund.
