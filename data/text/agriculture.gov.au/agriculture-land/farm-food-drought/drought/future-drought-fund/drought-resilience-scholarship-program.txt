The Future Drought Fund has partnered with Nuffield Australia to support farmers who want to learn more about drought resilience. Through this program, selected scholars travel across Australia and overseas to study how farmers can better prepare for drought and meet experts who are leading change in agriculture. Scholars share what they learn with their peers and communities to strengthen Australian agriculture.
The first stage of the program provided $1.6 million for 10 scholarships in 2024 and 2025 and supported all Nuffield scholars to take part in drought-related activities.
In 2024, the program received an additional $3.2 million to continue for four more years. From 2026 to 2029, up to 20 drought resilience scholarships will be awarded. All Nuffield scholars will also be supported to build their knowledge in drought resilience.

2024 Nuffield Australia Drought Resilience Scholars
- Caitlin Herbert (NSW) – exploring cattle feedlots role in ‘drought-proofing’ Australia’s livestock production.

- Ben Poschelk (QLD) – looking at building farmer drought resilience in the face of a changing climate.

The 2024 Nuffield drought resilience scholar cohort presented their research at Nuffield Australia’s national conference in September 2025. Their reports will be published in 2026.
