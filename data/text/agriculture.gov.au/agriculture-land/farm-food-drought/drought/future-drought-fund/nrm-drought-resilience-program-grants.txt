The Future Drought Fund is providing funds to build the capacity and resilience of soils and agricultural landscapes to underpin farm productivity through times of drought.
The grants are for innovative NRM practices, systems and approaches. These will improve natural capital and contribute towards landscape-scale drought resilience.
In our foundational year (2020/21), following a competitive process, we awarded 66 grants. Please see the successful grants table for full details.
A competitive grants process opened in late 2021 (and closed in early 2022), following consultation on how best to target the investment to achieve the desired transformational changes in land management. This will be under the new Drought Resilient Soils and Landscapes program, which builds on both Landscapes and Grants streams of the foundational NRM Drought Resilience program. To stay up to date , please register at the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry subscription centre to be kept informed of all FDF announcements.
