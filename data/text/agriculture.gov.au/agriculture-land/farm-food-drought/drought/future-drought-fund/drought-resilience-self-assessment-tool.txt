Decommissioning of the Drought Resilience Self-Assessment Tool
The Drought Resilience Self-Assessment Tool was decommissioned on 24 June 2024. Please visit Climate Services for Agriculture where you can access up-to-date climate information or the Future Drought Fund for further information on projects supported by the Future Drought Fund.
The Drought Resilience Self-Assessment Tool (DR.SAT) was one of two programs funded under the Better Climate Information theme of the Future Drought Fund. DR.SAT was a free resilience self-assessment tool that offered farm-scale analysis in financial, personal, social and environmental indicators. The 2023 Productivity Commission Review recommended DR.SAT be integrated into the Climate Services for Agriculture program.
The department is currently considering what elements of greatest value of DR.SAT could be integrated into Climate Services for Agriculture.
