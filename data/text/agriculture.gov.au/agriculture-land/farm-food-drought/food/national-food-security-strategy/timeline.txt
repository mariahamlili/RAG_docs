The strategy seeks to set a vision for a sustainable, resilient, and secure food system that serves all Australians from producers to consumers.
This timeline provides key milestones for developing the strategy.
Development of the National Food Security Strategy commences
Start of the public consultation process and release of Discussion Paper.
First meeting of the National Food Council with regular meetings throughout strategy development.
Co-design phase concludes.
Strategy drafting continues.
National Food Security Strategy released.
