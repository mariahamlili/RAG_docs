Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2016
The animal residue testing datasets 2015-16 outline testing that was carried out on animal products in the 2015-16 financial year, under the National Residue Survey.
Tables in each document include details of the:
- chemicals that were screened
- testing methodology
- analytical findings of the tests.
