Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2014
The animal residue testing datasets 2013–14 outline testing that was carried out on animal products in the 2013–14 financial year, under the National Residue Survey.
Tables in each document include details of the:
- chemicals that were screened
- testing methodology
- analytical findings of the tests.
