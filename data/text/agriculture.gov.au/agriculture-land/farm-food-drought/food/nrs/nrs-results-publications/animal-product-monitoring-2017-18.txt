Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2018
The animal residue testing datasets 2017-18 outline testing that was carried out on animal products in the 2017-18 financial year, under the National Residue Survey.
Tables in each document include details of the:
- chemicals that were screened
- testing methodology
- analytical findings of the tests.
