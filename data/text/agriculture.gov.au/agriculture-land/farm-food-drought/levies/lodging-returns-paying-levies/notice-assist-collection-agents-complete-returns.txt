If you are a collection agent, you may request information from a levy or export charge payer to complete your returns or make records.
Check the levy and export charge for your product to confirm the information you need.
Definitions
Collection agent is the person who collects and pays the levy or charge on behalf of the levy or export charge payer.
Levy or export charge payer is the person who is responsible for paying a levy or export charge on an agricultural product.
How to use the notice
Use the notice if you are unable to obtain the information you need from a levy or export charge payer. You will need to:
- download and complete the notice to assist collection agents to complete returns or make records.
- email, post or deliver the notice to the relevant person.
Levy or export charge payers who receive this notice are required by law to respond within 14 days of receiving the notice.
