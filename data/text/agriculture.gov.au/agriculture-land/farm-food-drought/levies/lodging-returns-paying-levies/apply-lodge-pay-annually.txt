An annual payment option only applies to some products.
You may be eligible to lodge and pay annually if you meet the dollar value or quantity threshold set for that product.
Check if you are eligible and how to apply.
How to apply
You must apply before the end of the first quarter or month in which levy or export charge is imposed.
To apply to lodge and/or pay annually:
- Check if your product meets the threshold limit
- Contact the levies team in your state or territory
- Download the Application to lodge and pay annually, or
- Download the Application to pay wine export charge annually
- Send your completed application to either:
Email (preferred): LeviesDebt@aff.gov.au
Post:
Levies Compliance Section
Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry
Locked Bag 4488
Kingston ACT 2604
Note: Please do not mark your envelope as ‘private’ or ‘confidential’.
We will assess your application and give you our decision, in writing, within 30 days of receiving the application.
You may contact us to discuss your application, or a decision that was made about your application, and we will help you meet your return and payment obligations.
Eligibility
You can apply to lodge and pay annually if you reasonably believe that you are unlikely to meet or exceed the threshold set for your product.
