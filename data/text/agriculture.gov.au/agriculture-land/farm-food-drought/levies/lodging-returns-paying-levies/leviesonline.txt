First time lodging with us
You will need a levies account number if you are lodging a return for the first time.
Once your request is approved, you will receive a levies account number (e.g. LRS12345).
For any help with the form, call Levies on 1800 020 619 (within Australia).
Use Levies Online
The fastest way to lodge your returns is via our levies online system.
You can also:
- make changes to returns
- manage communication preferences
- reset passwords and update contact information
- view payment history and download statements.
Where information in a return can’t be changed online, email us a signed copy of the amended return to: levies.management@aff.gov.au.
You can also contact the Levies team.
Two-factor authentication to keep you safe online
When you log in to Levies Online you will be given a security code via email and mobile. Codes are valid for 45 minutes and can only be used once.
Your code may not come through to you immediately. If you have not received a code within 20 minutes, try again or contact the Levies team.
