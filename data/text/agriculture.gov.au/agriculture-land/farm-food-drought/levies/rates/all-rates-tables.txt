How levy and export charge rates are set
Levy and export charge rates are set by the Australian primary industries that choose to invest in the levies system. The rates are established under legislation.
Levies and charges help to pay for:
- biosecurity activities and responses
- marketing
- research and development
- residue testing.
Check individual product pages for details of levy and charge on a specific product.
