For research and development corporations (RDCs) with established levy payer registers.
RDCs can use this portal to access details of levy and charge rates, levy and charge payers and disbursements.
Intend to use or disclose the levy payer information from your register?
See more on the use and disclosure of levy payer information.
Registration process
You will need to register first to gain access to the portal.
Contact us to create a portal user account:
- Phone: 1800 020 619
- Email: levies.management@aff.gov.au
Features
The levy recipient portal allows you to:
- manage your account details
- access disbursement reports
- view and extract levy payer data
- view and extract collection agent data
- view levy rate history.
