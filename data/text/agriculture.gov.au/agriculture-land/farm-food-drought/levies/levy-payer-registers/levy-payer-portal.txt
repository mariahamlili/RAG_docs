Levy and charge payers can use this portal to access and manage the information we collect.
You can only access information about yourself and the levies or charges you pay on the portal, if the research and development corporation (RDC) your levies are paid to has established a levy payer register for that commodity. This means that some of the levies you pay may not be included in the portal because the relevant RDC has not established a register for that commodity.
Registration process
You can register for your account on the levy payer portal.
To protect your privacy, you will be asked to provide details of your levy transactions or documents to verify your identity.
Features
The levy payer portal gives you access to:
- view your levy amounts paid
- manage your personal details
- change the primary user on the account.
