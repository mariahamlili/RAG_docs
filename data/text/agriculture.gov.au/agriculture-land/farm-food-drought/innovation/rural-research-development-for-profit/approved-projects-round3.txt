Round three, Rural R&D for Profit summary of approved projects
On 4 May 2017, the Hon. Barnaby Joyce MP, the Minister for Agriculture, approved funding of more than $35.8 million from 2016-17 to 2019-20 for seven projects under round three of the Rural R&D for Profit Program. This funding will be matched by more than $59 million in cash and in-kind contributions by research and development corporations and their partners. The application period for the third round of funding opened on 6 October 2016, and closed 6 December 2016.
The successful projects will all contribute to the program objective – to realise productivity and profitability improvements for primary producers.
