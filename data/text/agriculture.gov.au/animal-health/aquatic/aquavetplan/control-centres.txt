Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2008
The Control Centres Management Manual is an integral part of the Australian Aquatic Veterinary
Emergency Plan (AQUAVETPLAN).
The manual outlines the organisational response during the investigational, alert, operational and
stand-down phases of an emergency aquatic animal disease incident, addressing legislative,
management and resource issues.
The manual lists the immediate duties of field officers, senior managers, the Chief Veterinary Officer (or
Director of Fisheries where appropriate) and other staff in each phase.
