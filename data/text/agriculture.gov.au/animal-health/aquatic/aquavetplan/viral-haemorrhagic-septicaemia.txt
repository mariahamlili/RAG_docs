Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2014
This disease strategy manual is an integral part of the Australian Aquatic Veterinary Emergency Plan
(AQUAVETPLAN).
The manual sets out the disease control principles for use in response to a suspected or confirmed
incursion of viral haemorrhagic septicaemia virus in Australia.
Viral haemorrhagic septicaemia virus has been isolated from approximately 80 different fish species
throughout the Northern Hemisphere, including North America, Asia and Europe. It causes fish kills in
wild fish and high mortality in affected farmed fish.
