Overview
With assistance from Animal Health Australia, the Australian Alpaca Association has launched an NLIS
for alpacas in a voluntary capacity.
The NLIS is designed to track devices and corresponding livestock movements for disease control,
biosecurity, food safety, market access and other industry-related purposes. NLIS for alpacas uses a
visually readable electronic tag that stays with each animal for life.
Project Lead: Australian Alpaca Association (AAA); Animal Health Australia
Project Contact: aha@animalhealthaustralia.com.au
Project Status: Active
Objective alignment:
7 – Improve the integrity of animal health systems.
Activity alignment:
7.1 – Develop cost-effective national traceability registers or systems for agreed animal industries
that do not have one.
7.3 – Use traceability systems to provide feedback to supply chain participants on animal health
outcomes.
Project updates
November 2025
The AAA released a suite of educational material for alpaca owners about how to use the NLIS. For
further information please see the AAA NLIS website.
