Overview
In 2023, AUSVETPLAN Response strategy: Lumpy skin disease Version 5.1 was published on the AHA
website. Updates included relevant learnings from Exercise LSD2, a government and industry exercise
held in December 2022 to test components of the previous version of the AUSVETPLAN Response
strategy: Lumpy skin disease.
Project Lead: AHA
Project Contact: aha@animalhealthaustralia.com.au
Project Status: Complete
Objective alignment:
1 – Improve Australia’s preparedness and ability to respond to emergency animal diseases (EAD).
Activity alignment:
1.1 – Continue to implement recommendations from emergency responses including EAD
incursions, COVID-19, previous EAD simulation exercises and recent natural disasters.
