Overview
This project aims to build on previous phases of the Australian Biosecurity Genomic Database (ABGD)
development project. Phase 3 aims to:
add additional viral subtypes
expand the database to include state based notifiable diseases
trial the inclusion of bacterial pathogens
determine a long-term solution for database maintenance.
Project Lead: AgriBio (Victoria); Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry
Project Contact: Animalhealthlaboratories@aff.gov.au
Project Status: Complete
Project Date: May 2024 to June 2025
Objective alignment:
2 – Improve Australia’s surveillance and diagnostic capacity for animal pests and diseases.
Activity alignment:
2.1 – Implement actions under National Animal Health Surveillance Business Plan and National
Animal Health Diagnostic Business Plan.
