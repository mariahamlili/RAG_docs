Vaughan JA, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for bovine trichomoniasis identifies and
describes laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing bovine trichomoniasis in
susceptible cattle.
This ASDT has been superseded by the Australian and New Zealand standard diagnostic procedure
(ANZSDP) for bovine trichomoniasis.
