This document details recommendations made at the SCAHLS Workshop—Molecular Diagnosis and
PCR-Related Technologies for Pathogen Detection in Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratories. These
support a national standard and guideline document should be prepared for adoption and use by
veterinary diagnostic laboratories within Australia and New Zealand.
