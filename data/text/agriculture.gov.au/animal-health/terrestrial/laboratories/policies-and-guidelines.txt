Australia has a favourable animal health status. We are free from many of the economically important
diseases that occur in other parts of the world.
Laboratory diagnostics are key components of Australia’s animal health system.
Quality assured diagnostics:
detect emergency and emerging animal diseases early
support the management of endemic pests and diseases
These activities underpin national and international trade in livestock products and help to safeguard
the economy, environment and health of Australians.
Our network
We are part of a national animal health laboratory network of state and territory governments and
CSIRO. It also includes universities and industry groups. The network undertakes and manages
laboratory diagnostic activities in Australia.
The coordination of this network’s functions enables Australia to meet international and domestic
requirements of diagnostic laboratory services for various trade and certification purposes.
