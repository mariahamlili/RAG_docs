Publication details
Axel Colling, 2010
Measurement uncertainty (MU) provides quantitative estimates of the level of confidence that a
laboratory has in the analytical precision of test results and is therefore an essential component of a
quality system for veterinary diagnostic testing laboratories. This publications outlines two methods of
estimating MU.
