The AHC Communiqué (previously known as the Vet Communiqué) is a newsletter produced bi-
annually by the Animal Health Committee (AHC) after each face-to-face meeting.
The aim of the AHC Communiqué is to provide information on AHC discussions on a range of national
animal health issues.
