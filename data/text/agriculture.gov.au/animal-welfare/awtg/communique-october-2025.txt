Options for the Development of National Standards and
Guidelines for Animal Welfare
The AWTG agreed to report on progress on AWTG’s work to improve the process for developing
national animal welfare standards and guidelines to senior officials.
Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for Livestock at
Processing Establishments
AWTG 25-05 discussed options to progress development of the processing standards, which is being led
by Queensland.
Australian Animal Welfare Standards and Guidelines for
Land Transport of Livestock – horses
Ministers continue to work towards national endorsement of amendments to the Australian Animal
Welfare Standards and Guidelines – Land Transport of Livestock to improve the welfare of horses.
Pig Welfare
AWTG continue to discuss details of a project by Australian Pork Limited to improve the welfare of pigs.
Virtual Fencing Technology
AWTG continues to work towards the finalisation of the Australian Animal Welfare Guide for Virtual
Fencing following additional feedback provided by jurisdictions.
Implementation of the Australian Animal Welfare
Standards and Guidelines for Poultry into state and
territory legislation
AWTG noted updates from jurisdictions on current progress to implement the poultry standards into
state and territory legislation.
Renewed Australian Animal Welfare Strategy (AAWS)
AWTG noted an update from the Commonwealth that chapter drafting for the Livestock and Production
and Aquatic Animals chapters of the Australian Animal Welfare Strategy continues.
