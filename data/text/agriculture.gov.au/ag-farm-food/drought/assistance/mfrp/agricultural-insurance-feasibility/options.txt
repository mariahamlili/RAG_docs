Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, September 2012
Australian agriculture operates in uncertain climatic and market conditions. Climate change is anticipated to increase the frequency of extreme weather events, including drought. Australian farmers must constantly manage the risks of changing global market settings and volatile domestic growing conditions.
