Publication details
Department of Agriculture and Water Resources, 2016
This disease strategy manual is an integral part of the Australian Aquatic Veterinary Emergency Plan (AQUAVETPLAN).
The manual sets out the disease control principles for use in response to a suspected or confirmed incursion of whirling disease in Australia.
