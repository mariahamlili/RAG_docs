Publication details
Barr DA and O’Rourke MD, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for avian influenza identify and describe laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing infection caused by avian influenza viruses in susceptible domestic poultry species. This version has been superseded by the latest version of the Australian and New Zealand standard diagnostic procedure (ANZSDP) for avian influenza.
