Publication details
Hornitzky MAZ and Glastonbury JRW, 1986
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for enterotoxaemia identifies and describes laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing enterotoxaemia caused by Clostridium perfringens in susceptible animals.
