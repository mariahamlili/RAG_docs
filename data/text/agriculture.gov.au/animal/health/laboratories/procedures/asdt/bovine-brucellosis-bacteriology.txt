Publication details
Corner LA and Alton GG, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for bovine brucellosis: bacteriology identifies and describes bacteriological methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing infection caused by Brucella spp. in susceptible domestic animals.
