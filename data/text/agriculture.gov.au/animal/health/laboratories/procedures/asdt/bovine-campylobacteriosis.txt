Publication details
Hum S and McInnes A, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for bovine campylobacteriosis identifies and describes laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing bovine venereal campylobacteria in susceptible domestic cattle. This version has been superseded by the latest version of the Australian and New Zealand standard diagnostic procedure (ANZSDP0 for bovine genital campylobacteriosis.
