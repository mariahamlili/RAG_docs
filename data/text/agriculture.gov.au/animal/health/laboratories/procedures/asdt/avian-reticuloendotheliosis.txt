Bagust TJ and Ignjatovic J, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for avian reticuloendotheliosis identifies and describes laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing avian reticuloendotheliosis in susceptible poultry.
