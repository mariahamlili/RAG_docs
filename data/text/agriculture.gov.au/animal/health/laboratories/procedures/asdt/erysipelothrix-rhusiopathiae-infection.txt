Publication details
Cross GM and Eamens GJ, 1987
The Australian standard diagnostic techniques (ASDT) for Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae infection identifies and describes laboratory methods that assist in detecting and diagnosing infection caused by Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae in pigs.
