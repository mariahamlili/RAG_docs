Publication details
Handlinger J, 2008
Samples of finfish, water and other material intended for submission to a laboratory for examination or necropsy to obtain a diagnosis of disease must be adequate and appropriate for the purpose. Fish show a limited range of clinical signs and gross lesions, so the quality of the samples to enable differentiation of diseases with similar presentation is crucial. A full history should accompany the samples. The specific methods for collecting, preparing and submitting the samples differ according to the examinations needed. Protocols for all methods are described.
