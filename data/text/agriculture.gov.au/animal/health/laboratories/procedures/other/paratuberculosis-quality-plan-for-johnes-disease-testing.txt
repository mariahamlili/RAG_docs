Gwozdz J, 2005
The Australian and New Zealand standard diagnostic procedure (ANZSDP) for Johne’s disease quality plan provides guidelines for consistent standardisation of the laboratory tests used for national Johne’s disease programs in Australia. It has been superseded by the latest version of 2010.
