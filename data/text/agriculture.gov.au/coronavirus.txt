We are working to keep our agriculture industries strong and safeguard our environment and heritage during the COVID-19 outbreak.
We know the importance of our agriculture sectors. Feeding our nation is an essential service. We want to ensure high-quality produce can reach domestic and overseas markets.
We play an essential role in preserving our unique biodiversity. We want to ensure we can protect our natural resources and heritage, and the public who want to enjoy them.
